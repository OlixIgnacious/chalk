# Step 3 for news: the script

Read `core/references/step-3-script.md` for the two-layer form; this adds the news rules.

- **Attribute in the line** (W3): "The council says the library opens until 10 pm." A line that carries a
  number or a quotation names its reporter in that line or the one just before, in the same scene.
- **Display numbers as the reporting gives them** ("1,200", "10 pm"), spoken naturally ("twelve hundred").
- **No hype** (W4) in the narration or the title.
- **The last line** points to the sources (W5) and cites `[pipeline]`: "Sources are in the description."
- **What is not known** is said as not known ("the council has not said how much it costs"), cited to the
  block that says so; never filled in.

**Gate:** `chalk eval <out> --text` passes the news group and G1.
