# Step 2 for a skit: the outline

Write the cast first: who is on stage, each one's voice (`cast` in the manifest), and their role: the one who
gets it wrong, the one who knows. Then the beats:

| Beat | Does |
|---|---|
| Setup | a situation the viewer recognises, from the source's idea |
| Misunderstanding | one character applies the idea wrongly, or the wrong idea |
| Turn | the other corrects it, with the source's words |
| Button | a last exchange that lands the idea as a joke |

A Short is one skit of 20–50 s; a long video strings several skits, one idea each, as parts.

**Gate:** each beat has its block ids; every speaker has a voice; the correction in the turn cites the block
that says what is right.
