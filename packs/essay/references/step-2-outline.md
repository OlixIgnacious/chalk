# Step 2 for an essay: the outline

Write `outline.md` with a parts table (`| # | Title | Teaches |`) and, for the long shape, `video.json`:
`title`, `thesis` (one sentence, ≤ 25 words), `thesis_source`, `pack: "essay"`, `segments`, and `theme`
(a calm one: `dusk`, `poster`, `ink`, `apple`, or the channel's).

A long essay has three to six parts:

| Part | Does |
|---|---|
| Opening image | something the viewer can see, from the source; no thesis yet |
| The problem | what gets in the way, in the viewer's own terms |
| The turn | the idea, said plainly; the quotation, if there is one, lives here |
| Practice | what the viewer does with it: one small, concrete thing |
| Return | the opening image again, changed by the idea |

A Short is the turn and the return in one part: open on the image, say the idea, end on the image.

**Gate:** one thesis; the opening and the return named and sharing their image; every quotation listed with
its block id and, from `facts.md`, its verdict (anything not `supported` is cut or loses its name now).
