# poster

**For:** focus.

## Essence

Flat silkscreen colours, a grain finish, big type with generous air, one line per screen: for quotations and manifestos.

## What it is not

Not a meme: no impact font, no white outline, no all-caps shouting.

## Colour

- `INK` #F1E8D6 the ground; `INK_ALT` #E9DDC4 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #E4572E: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #1D2A3A text; `MUTED` #4E5B6B secondary text; `HINT` #B9AE97 and `BORDER` #D6CAB0 structure.

## Type

Barlow for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

One line rises; it holds; the next replaces it. Reveal style: `rise`. Finish: grain.

## Pitfalls

- A quotation's attribution is part of the line (the essay pack's Y2), set smaller under it, never dropped.
- Grain on a cream ground lowers contrast: navy text only, 600 weight or more.
