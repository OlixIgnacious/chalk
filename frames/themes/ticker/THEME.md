# ticker

**For:** news.

## Essence

An amber LED ticker and a terminal-style status bar as the chrome, beside midnight: facts type in like a wire.

## What it is not

Not a stock-market screen: no tickers of numbers the reporting does not give.

## Colour

- `INK` #0B0B0B the ground; `INK_ALT` #151515 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #FFB000: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #F5F5F5 text; `MUTED` #A3A3A3 secondary text; `HINT` #3A3A3A and `BORDER` #262626 structure.

## Type

Inter for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Facts type in; attributions fade under them. Reveal style: `typed`. Finish: none.

## Pitfalls

- Typed reveals take time: a long line types for over a second, so cue it early (R6 times it).
- The date label is the news pack's W2: keep it on screen.
