# firefly

**For:** focus.

## Essence

Points of light drifting on near-black, glowing as they pass; type fades in among them.

## What it is not

Not fireworks: slow, few, warm.

## Colour

- `INK` #05060A the ground; `INK_ALT` #0C0E16 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #FFD27A: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #F4EEDC text; `MUTED` #9A978C secondary text; `HINT` #3A3A40 and `BORDER` #1E1F26 structure.

## Type

Inter for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

The field drifts all frame; text fades slowly; long holds. Reveal style: `fade`. Finish: vignette. Field: {"kind": "firefly", "count": 30, "color": "#FFD27A", "size": [2, 6], "opacity": 0.7}.

## Pitfalls

- More than ~40 points becomes noise.
- The points gathering into a word is a later mechanism: today they drift.
