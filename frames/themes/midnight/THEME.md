# midnight

**For:** news.

## Essence

Deep navy with cyan and violet: the news look, a dated label on top, facts set like a wire report.

## What it is not

Not a dashboard: no fake tickers or numbers that are not in the source.

## Colour

- `INK` #0A1024 the ground; `INK_ALT` #121B36 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` #22D3EE the accent: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #EEF2FF text; `MUTED` #8B95B5 secondary text; `HINT` #4A5578 and `BORDER` #1F2A4A structure.

## Type

Inter for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Things rise into place (`rise`), values pop, lines are drawn. One change at a time.

## Pitfalls

- Cyan on navy is low contrast for thin weights: 600 or more for body text.
- The label's date must be the reporting's date (the news pack's W2).
