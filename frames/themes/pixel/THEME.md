# pixel

**For:** skits.

## Essence

A 16-bit palette, a bitmap face, reveals on hard cuts.

## What it is not

Not a game UI: no lives counter, no score that is not in the source.

## Colour

- `INK` #1A1C2C the ground; `INK_ALT` #29366F panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #FFCD75: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #F4F4F4 text; `MUTED` #94B0C2 secondary text; `HINT` #566C86 and `BORDER` #333C57 structure.

## Type

Press Start 2P for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Things appear in one step; nothing eases. Reveal style: `step`. Finish: none.

## Pitfalls

- Press Start 2P is wide and tall: 40 px reads, 28 px is the floor; keep lines short.
- The cast as sprites is a later addition: the stage uses the cut-out cast until then.
