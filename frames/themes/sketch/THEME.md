# sketch

**For:** skits.

## Essence

Black line on white paper, no accent colour: a cartoon skit's whiteboard.

## What it is not

Not a lesson slide: no colour coding to lean on, so the drawing must carry meaning.

## Colour

- `INK` #FFFFFF the ground; `INK_ALT` #F4F4F4 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` #111111 the accent: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #111111 text; `MUTED` #777777 secondary text; `HINT` #BBBBBB and `BORDER` #DDDDDD structure.

## Type

Barlow for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Things rise into place (`rise`), values pop, lines are drawn. One change at a time.

## Pitfalls

- With no accent, a marked cell needs a heavier border, not a fill.
- White frames flare on a dark phone: keep scenes short.
