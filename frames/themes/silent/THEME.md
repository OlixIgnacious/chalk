# silent

**For:** skits.

## Essence

Black and white with grain and flicker: a silent film; the spoken line also as an intertitle card.

## What it is not

Not sepia nostalgia: black and white, not brown.

## Colour

- `INK` #0E0E0E the ground; `INK_ALT` #1B1B1B panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #EDE6D6: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #F2EEE4 text; `MUTED` #A39E93 secondary text; `HINT` #4A4740 and `BORDER` #3A3833 structure.

## Type

Barlow for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Hard cuts (step), no easing; a seeded flicker. Reveal style: `step`. Finish: grain, flicker, vignette.

## Pitfalls

- The ornate intertitle frame and the iris wipe are later components: cards are plain until then.
- Flicker over a whole skit tires: keep a skit under a minute in this theme.
