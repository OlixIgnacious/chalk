# ink

**For:** focus.

## Essence

Ink on paper: strokes with a soft bleed, a lot of empty space, a seal-red accent.

## What it is not

Not calligraphy cosplay: no fake Chinese or Japanese characters as decoration.

## Colour

- `INK` #F5F0E6 the ground; `INK_ALT` #FBF8F1 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #B3261E: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #1A1A1A text; `MUTED` #5C5850 secondary text; `HINT` #C9C1B1 and `BORDER` #E2DACB structure.

## Type

Caveat for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Drawn on with a soft blur that settles (the bleed); long pauses. Reveal style: `draw`. Finish: paper.

## Pitfalls

- The seal red is for one mark per screen.
- Caveat is not a brush face: keep it large; a brush face is a later addition.
