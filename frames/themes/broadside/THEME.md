# broadside

**For:** explaining.

## Essence

Dark, high-contrast, one orange accent, lower-case display titles in Barlow, generous negative space, line-drawn diagrams.

## What it is not

Not a blackboard, not a neon sign: no texture, no glow, no second accent.

## Colour

- `INK` #111111 the ground; `INK_ALT` #1A1A18 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` #E85D26 the accent: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #F0ECE5 text; `MUTED` #888880 secondary text; `HINT` #505048 and `BORDER` #282826 structure.

## Type

Barlow for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Things rise into place (`rise`), values pop, lines are drawn. One change at a time.

## Pitfalls

- Orange is for the one thing the narration is tracking; two orange things on screen means neither leads.
- Lower-case titles keep O(1), SQL and [1, 2, 3, 1] in their own case: do not force them.
