# bauhaus

**For:** explaining.

## Essence

Primary colours and simple shapes standing for the parts of an idea, moving on a grid: for principles and mechanisms.

## What it is not

Not decoration: a shape stands for something the narration names, or it is not drawn.

## Colour

- `INK` #F2EBDD the ground; `INK_ALT` #FFFFFF panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #C62828: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #1A1A1A text; `MUTED` #4A4A4A secondary text; `HINT` #2E5BBA and `BORDER` #C99A06 structure.

## Type

Inter for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Shapes pop into place; one colour per role, kept for the whole video. Reveal style: `rise`. Finish: none.

## Pitfalls

- Three colours means three roles: write them in outline.md and keep them.
- Blue (HINT) is a role here, not a hint colour: do not use it for secondary text.
