# apple

**For:** explaining, focus.

## Essence

A dark keynote: true black, white type in Inter, one blue-to-pink gradient through the words that matter.

## What it is not

Not a rainbow: the gradient is for one word or one result per screen.

## Colour

- `INK` #000000 the ground; `INK_ALT` #1D1D1F panels on it.
- `ORANGE` #2997FF the accent: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #F5F5F7 text; `MUTED` #86868B secondary text; `HINT` #424245 and `BORDER` #2D2D2F structure.

## Type

Inter for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Things rise into place (`rise`), values pop, lines are drawn. One change at a time.

## Pitfalls

- A gradient on small text turns to mud on a phone: only on 56 px or more.
- Pure black shows banding in a dim room: keep big flat areas in INK_ALT.
