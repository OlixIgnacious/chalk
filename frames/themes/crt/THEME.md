# crt

**For:** explaining, news.

## Essence

Phosphor green on near-black, scanlines, a terminal face; text types itself in, glyph by glyph.

## What it is not

Not a hacker movie: no matrix rain, no fake command noise that is not in the source.

## Colour

- `INK` #050805 the ground; `INK_ALT` #0B120B panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #B7FFC0: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #33FF66 text; `MUTED` #1FB04A secondary text; `HINT` #145A26 and `BORDER` #0F3A1A structure.

## Type

VT323 for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Typed (glyph by glyph, a step per character); no easing; one line at a time. Reveal style: `typed`. Finish: scanlines.

## Pitfalls

- VT323 is a bitmap-like face: 48 px or more, or it shimmers under the scanlines.
- Green on black tires the eye over minutes: a long video in crt needs a calmer theme for its trace parts.
- No second colour: the accent is brighter green, not orange.
