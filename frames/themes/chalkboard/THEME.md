# chalkboard

**For:** explaining, skits.

## Essence

Deep slate green with fine grain; chalk white for structure, one yellow chalk for what the lesson tracks; a hand face; nothing fades in: things are written.

## What it is not

Not a classroom prop: no wooden frame, no eraser smudges as decoration, no apple on the desk.

## Colour

- `INK` #1F2B24 the ground; `INK_ALT` #26342C panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #F2C14E: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #EDEBE4 text; `MUTED` #A7B0A9 secondary text; `HINT` #4A5A50 and `BORDER` #3A4A41 structure.

## Type

Caveat for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Written on at hand speed (draw-on); lines drawn along their path; one wipe clears a board. Reveal style: `draw`. Finish: grain.

## Pitfalls

- Caveat is narrow and light: 56 px or more for anything read, and never for code (code stays IBM Plex Mono).
- Grain over small chalk text reads as noise: keep labels at 32 px or more.
- Two chalk colours at once on one idea is one too many.
