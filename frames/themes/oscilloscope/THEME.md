# oscilloscope

**For:** explaining.

## Essence

A dark grid, one beam colour, curves that trace themselves, readouts in a mono face: for math and anything plotted.

## What it is not

Not a sci-fi screen: one beam, no fake waveforms the source does not give.

## Colour

- `INK` #06100D the ground; `INK_ALT` #0B1A15 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #39FF9F: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #D8FFEE text; `MUTED` #6FBFA0 secondary text; `HINT` #1E4D3D and `BORDER` #12352A structure.

## Type

Inter for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Traced by a beam (a glow that settles); readouts type in. Reveal style: `beam`. Finish: grid.

## Pitfalls

- The glow blooms on small text: beam reveals for curves and headings, not for body text.
- A plotted value must come from an example script, like every number.
