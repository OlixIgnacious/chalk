# yellow

**For:** explaining.

## Essence

White and #FFD60A on near-black, heavy Barlow: a thumbnail-first look that carries into the video.

## What it is not

Not a warning sign: yellow is for the word that matters, never a whole panel.

## Colour

- `INK` #0D0D0D the ground; `INK_ALT` #1D1D1D panels on it.
- `ORANGE` #FFD60A the accent: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #FFFFFF text; `MUTED` #8A8A8A secondary text; `HINT` #3A3A3A and `BORDER` #2A2A2A structure.

## Type

Barlow for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Things rise into place (`rise`), values pop, lines are drawn. One change at a time.

## Pitfalls

- Yellow text on white captions loses its point: captions stay white with the yellow word only.
- The bottom right 220×120 is YouTube's duration timer on thumbnails.
