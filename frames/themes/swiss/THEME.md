# swiss

**For:** explaining.

## Essence

A strict grid, one signal red, type leading; every move on a beat. broadside and yellow are near it; this is the cleaner, light version.

## What it is not

Not a corporate slide: no icons, no stock photos, no drop shadows.

## Colour

- `INK` #F4F1EA the ground; `INK_ALT` #FFFFFF panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #E30613: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #111111 text; `MUTED` #5A5A5A secondary text; `HINT` #B5B0A6 and `BORDER` #D8D3C8 structure.

## Type

Inter for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Rises on the beat of the narration; nothing moves that is not being said. Reveal style: `rise`. Finish: none.

## Pitfalls

- Align everything to the 104 px margin and the 48 px grid: a stray x reads as a mistake here.
- Red only for the one thing tracked.
