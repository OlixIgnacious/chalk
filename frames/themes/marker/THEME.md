# marker

**For:** explaining, skits.

## Essence

Marker on white paper: black for structure, one red with a fixed meaning; boxes and arrows drawn by hand; a paper finish. sketch is the version without colour; both stay.

## What it is not

Not a whiteboard photo: no glare, no ghost writing from an earlier lesson.

## Colour

- `INK` #FAFAF7 the ground; `INK_ALT` #FFFFFF panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #D7263D: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #1B1B1B text; `MUTED` #555555 secondary text; `HINT` #BDBDBD and `BORDER` #D9D9D4 structure.

## Type

Caveat for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Drawn on at hand speed; a box draws its outline before its content. Reveal style: `draw`. Finish: paper.

## Pitfalls

- White frames flare on a dark phone: keep scenes short and captions dark.
- The red is for one meaning (wrong, or the thing tracked): never both in one video.
- Caveat for headings only; body text in Inter reads faster.
