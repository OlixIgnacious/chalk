# neon

**For:** focus, news.

## Essence

Neon-tube type and lines with a glow and a faint flicker, on a dark wall: for an energetic opener.

## What it is not

Not a nightclub: one tube colour plus white, no rainbow.

## Colour

- `INK` #0B0716 the ground; `INK_ALT` #140C24 panels on it.
- `ORANGE` (the accent slot) #FF3EA5: the one thing the narration is tracking.
- `CREAM` #E9F9FF text; `MUTED` #A79BD0 secondary text; `HINT` #3B2F5C and `BORDER` #2A2046 structure.

## Type

Inter for display; IBM Plex Mono for code and labels. Headlines 60–96 px, body 40 px or more, labels 28 px or more.

## Motion

Tubes light along their path; a faint seeded flicker over the frame. Reveal style: `draw`. Finish: glow, flicker, vignette.

## Pitfalls

- Flicker is on the timeline and seeded: never add a CSS animation.
- Pink on dark purple is low contrast for body text: CREAM for anything read.
