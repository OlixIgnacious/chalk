What chalk does

Turn a document into a video. Every line cites its source.

Give chalk a chapter, a PDF or your own script. It checks the facts, writes the narration and animates it, and every sentence points back to the paragraph it came from.

How it stays honest

Four rules every build keeps.

GROUNDED

No line without a source.

Every narrated line and on-screen claim cites a paragraph of the source. A line with no source fails the gate.

The library now opens until 9 pm on weekdays.¶ 3
The change starts on 1 December.¶ 3
It was the most requested change ever.no source
CHECKED AT THE SOURCE

The source gets checked too.

Once per version of the source, every number, date, superlative and quotation gets a verdict and its evidence. A contradicted claim stops the build until the source is fixed or you waive it.

COMPUTED, NOT INVENTED

Numbers come from running code.

Every derived number comes from an example script that ships with the build. Code on screen is the source block, character for character.

REVIEWED BEFORE RENDERED

You approve before anything renders.

The pipeline stops after the scripts for a human review. Fixing the teaching takes minutes; re-rendering takes much longer.

Any genre. The skit pack's rule: every line has a speaker, every speaker a voice, and they take turns.
